Made for the professional set

Night Haze delivers the volumetric light and atmospheric depth cinematographers, lighting directors and event producers chase. It gives a consistent, long-lasting mist that turns flat spaces into textured, striking environments.

The fluid is a clean-burning blend of vegetable glycerin and propylene glycol. It creates a rich atmosphere without leaving heavy residue on cinema lenses, sensors or event equipment, and its dark, minimal packaging looks at home on any set.
